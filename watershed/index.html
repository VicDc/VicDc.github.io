Watershed-AI

A terrain-analysis pipeline that takes a digital elevation model and finds the best point to inject water for a flood scenario: not the highest point, but the one that floods the largest area. Inspired by the problem Real Ice tackles in the Arctic, where to pump seawater onto ice to thicken it.

Method

  1. Ingestion. Load and clean the elevation grid from a public DEM (Copernicus GLO-30).
  2. Candidate selection. Local maxima filtered by prominence, never a plain elevation argmax: the highest point is rarely the best one.
  3. Flood simulation. Per candidate, a cellular-automata water spread over the depression-filled DEM (priority-flood), producing Area(t) and Area(Volume) curves.
  4. Ranking & output. Comparison chart, hypsometric map with water overlay, 2D and 3D time-lapse animations over satellite imagery.
Honest scope. The core is deterministic NumPy/SciPy raster computation: there is no machine-learning or LLM component, and it is an exploratory tool, not a production flood-forecasting system. Results based on 30m public DEMs are flagged as resolution-limited by design.

Principles

Computable ground truth should be computed, not delegated to a model.
  • Deterministic where it matters. Financial figures in Business Plan AI are re-derived by pure Python, and charts are rendered by code from structured specs. The LLM proposes, arithmetic verifies.
  • Honest failure over confident fiction. Sherpa falls back to conversation when no source clears the retrieval threshold. Non-convergent plans ship with a manual-review banner, not a fabricated result.
  • Metrics on the hard corpus. 62% mAP50 is reported on the full heterogeneous test set, not a narrower favourable slice.
  • Local-first by default. Ollama and LM Studio inference, Milvus snapshots, on-device pipelines. Data stays where it belongs.

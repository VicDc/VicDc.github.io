A guided IntakeAgent normalizes free-text input into a structured business profile. Six specialist agents (Vision, Market, Team, Setup, Financial, Funding) then draft their sections under an Orchestrator that applies six LLM-based consistency checks and issues targeted revisions, up to three cycles, before a separate ReportWriter produces the final document. The whole system runs on local inference (LM Studio or Ollama, Gemma) with no cloud dependency, so a business idea never leaves the machine.

It reuses the same BaseAgent/Orchestrator pattern as Sherpa Alzheimer, with no agentic framework dependency: the orchestration loop is explicit, inspectable Python.

The verification layer

Computable ground truth should be computed, not delegated to a model.

LLMs are unreliable at arithmetic, and a business plan is full of arithmetic. So after the agents converge, a pure-Python verification layer with no model in the loop re-derives every computable figure from the declared inputs: unit margin, break-even point, capital totals, funding coverage, revenue against addressable market. Any value diverging beyond 5% tolerance is flagged, and flagged rows export to XLSX for human review.
